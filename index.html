Topographical Effects of Materials on Aerodynamics

Does the surface finish of GT3 racecar bodywork matter aerodynamically? I ran seven steady-state CFD simulations in SimScale (k-ω SST turbulence model, Re ≈ 3.8 × 10⁶), converting published roughness values to sand-grain roughness with the Nikuradse relation and changing nothing between runs but the wall roughness. Drag rose 124% from gloss carbon fiber (Cd 0.021) to matte vinyl wrap (Cd 0.047), with a clean performance threshold separating gloss from matte finishes.

Bitcoin: How the Industry Opened a Gateway to Criminal Activity

My first research project traced how the Silk Road marketplace shaped Bitcoin's early price history. Through document analysis of sources including RAND, WIRED, and the Fordham Journal of Corporate & Financial Law, I followed Bitcoin from roughly $0.30 in January 2011 to about $1,100 by November 2013 — Silk Road activity reached roughly 20% of Bitcoin's economic activity at its peak, and the price collapsed ~81% immediately after the FBI seized the site. I estimated the marketplace drove 5–15% of Bitcoin's price movement across the period.
